## 2. Typography

### Font Families
- **Primary Font:** Inter (or system sans-serif: Segoe UI, SF Pro)
- **Monospace (Data/Metrics):** JetBrains Mono (or Consolas, Courier New)

### Hierarchy

| Element | Weight | Size (PPT) | Size (Word) | Letter Spacing |
|---------|--------|------------|-------------|----------------|
| **H1 - Main Title** | Bold (700) | 44-60pt | 28-32pt | Tight (-0.025em) |
| **H2 - Section Title** | Bold (700) | 32-40pt | 22-24pt | Tight |
| **H3 - Subsection** | Semibold (600) | 24-28pt | 16-18pt | Normal |
| **Body** | Regular (400) | 16-18pt | 11-12pt | Normal |
| **Caption/Label** | Medium (500) | 12-14pt | 9-10pt | Wide (0.05em), UPPERCASE |
| **Data/Numbers** | Monospace | 18-24pt | 12-14pt | Tight (-0.01em) |

## 3. Logo Usage

### Logo Components
- **Icon:** Target/crosshair symbol (use `logo.png` from `/public/images/`)
- **Wordmark:** "TrueAim" in bold + ".ai" in Ocean Blue (`#2563EB`)

### Logo Placement
- Top-left of slides/documents
- Minimum clear space: Height of the "T" in TrueAim on all sides
- Icon can be placed in a white rounded box when on dark backgrounds

### Tagline
**"Swiss Precision. Auditable AI."**

## 4. Visual Style

### Dark Theme (Recommended for PPT)
- Background: `#0B0C15` (Dark Navy)
- Cards/Panels: Semi-transparent white overlay (8-12% opacity) with subtle blur effect (glassmorphism)
- Borders: White at 12% opacity (`rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`)
- Corner radius: 12-24px for cards/containers

### Light Theme (Recommended for Word)
- Background: White (`#FFFFFF`)
- Cards: Slate 100 (`#F1F5F9`)
- Borders: Slate 200 (`#E2E8F0`)
- Text: Slate 950 for headlines, Slate 800 for body

### Gradient Accents
For visual interest, use subtle gradient overlays:
```
Linear gradient: Ocean Blue (#2563EB) → Cyan (#06B6D4) → Teal (#14B8A6)
```

## 5. Slide Templates (PPT)

### Title Slide
- Dark navy background (`#0B0C15`)
- Large white headline (Bold, 44-60pt)
- Highlighted words in Ocean Blue
- Subtle aurora gradient orbs in corners (30-40% opacity, blurred)

### Content Slide
- Dark background
- Section title in white (Bold, 32pt)
- Body text in 70% white
- Use glass panels for content blocks
- Ocean Blue accent bar on left or top

### Data/Metrics Slide
- Use monospace font for numbers
- Green (`#22C55E`) for positive metrics
- Use cards with glassmorphism effect

### Process/Pipeline Slide
- Horizontal flow with icons
- Connect steps with gradient lines (border → Ocean Blue)
- Use Aurora colors for icon backgrounds

## 6. Document Templates (Word)

### Headers
- Logo top-left
- Ocean Blue accent line below header
- Page numbers in Slate 600

### Body
- Inter or Segoe UI, 11pt
- Line height: 1.5
- Paragraph spacing: 12pt after

### Headings
- H1: 24pt, Bold, Slate 950
- H2: 18pt, Semibold, Ocean Blue
- H3: 14pt, Semibold, Slate 800

### Callout Boxes
- Background: `#EFF6FF` (Blue 50)
- Border-left: 4px solid `#2563EB`
- Padding: 16px

### Tables
- Header row: Ocean Blue background, white text
- Alternating rows: White / Slate 100
- Borders: Slate 200

---

## 7. Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use generous white space
- Apply tight letter-spacing on headlines
- Use Ocean Blue as the primary accent color
- Keep the dark theme for presentations (premium feel)
- Use green only for success/positive metrics

### Don't
- Use pink (legacy color, replaced with blue)
- Mix multiple accent colors in one section
- Use low-contrast text combinations
- Add gradients to body text
- Overuse the aurora effects (keep subtle)
